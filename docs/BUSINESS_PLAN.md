## 1. Executive Summary & Investment Memorandum

### 1.1 Company Overview
**LadderPass** (`ladderpass.co.uk`) is the UK’s premier statutory-grade financial decision modeling suite. Built specifically to eliminate the critical friction and knowledge gaps across major UK life milestones, LadderPass provides consumers with mathematical clarity across the six rungs of the British financial ladder:
1. **Property Ladder (`/` & `/city/[slug]`):** 40-year mortgage amortizations, First-Time Buyer (FTB) Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) relief cliff edges, and true buy-vs-rent breakevens across 19 UK regional metropolitan hubs.
2. **Tax & High Earner Ladder (`/tax`):** The 60% marginal tax trap (£100k–£125k), High Income Child Benefit Charge (HICBC) taper, 30-hour free childcare cliff edge, and Self-Invested Personal Pension (SIPP) salary sacrifice optimization.
3. **EV Mobility Ladder (`/ev`):** HMRC Benefit-in-Kind (BiK) rate transitions (2% to 5%), smart off-peak charging economics, and employer salary sacrifice vs private PCP and cash purchase modeling.
4. **Student Loan Ladder (`/student-loan`):** Plan 2 vs Plan 5 statutory write-off modeling (30 vs 40 years) and mortgage borrowing multiplier drag.
5. **Buy-to-Let SPV Ladder (`/property/spv`):** Section 24 mortgage interest relief restrictions, 19% Corporation Tax, and the 5% Higher Rates for Additional Dwellings (HRAD) Stamp Duty surcharge.
6. **Retirement & FIRE Ladder (`/retirement`):** Flexible drawdown vs lifetime annuity benchmarking, 25% Pension Commencement Lump Sum (PCLS), State Pension bridge modeling, and post-2027 Pension Inheritance Tax (IHT) integration.

Tip-jar monetization signals hobbyist software, destroying conversion while yielding negligible returns (£30–£50/month per 10,000 visits).

Cheap, deceptive affiliate lead traps are being eliminated. Intermediaries require pre-qualified, scenario-educated borrowers who understand their Loan-to-Value (LTV), loan requirements, and statutory costs.

## 2. Macro Market Analysis: TAM, SAM, & SOM

### 2.1 The UK Mortgage Lending Market
* **Annual Lending Volumes:** According to UK Finance and Bank of England data, total gross UK mortgage lending reached **£260 billion in 2025** and is projected to expand to **£300 billion in 2026**.
* **First-Time Buyer (FTB) Surge:** First-time buyers account for **52.8% of all UK mortgage sales** (over 378,000 transactions annually). Median FTB Loan-to-Value (LTV) sits at **85.6%**, with average Loan-to-Income (LTI) multipliers at **3.6x**.
* **Refinancing Wave:** Over **£77 billion** in external remortgages and **£261 billion** in internal product transfers mature annually, creating continuous demand for rate stress-testing.
* **Broker Intermediation Rate:** Over **84% of all UK residential mortgages** are originated via independent mortgage intermediaries, creating a massive, recurring demand for qualified customer introductions.

### 2.2 The UK High-Earner Wealth & Tax Demographic
* **The £100k+ Tax Trap Cohort:** HMRC income tax statistics confirm that over **1.6 million UK taxpayers** earn between £100,000 and £150,000. 
* **Statutory Clawback Impact:** This demographic faces the 60% effective marginal income tax rate (tapering of the £12,570 Personal Allowance between £100k and £125,140) combined with the abrupt loss of 30 hours of free childcare (worth up to £10,000 net per child).
* **Wealth Management Demand:** The UK Wealth Management sector oversees **£1.2 trillion in Assets Under Management (AUM)**. IFAs pay premium fees (£189–£319+ per enquiry on platforms like VouchedFor and Unbiased) to connect with high earners needing immediate pension salary sacrifice and tax restructuring.

### 2.3 The UK EV Salary Sacrifice Boom
* **Statutory Incentives:** HMRC Benefit-in-Kind (BiK) company car rates remain locked at **2% through 2024/25, rising gently to 3% (2025/26), 4% (2026/27), and 5% (2027/28)**. Compared to personal 40%–45% income tax brackets, salary sacrificing an EV saves an employee £200–£450 net per month.
* **Corporate Provider Expansion:** Providers such as Octopus EV, The Electric Car Scheme, and Tusker are aggressively onboarding corporate schemes, offering introducers substantial recurring commissions and per-vehicle delivery bounties (£250–£500 per corporate onboarding).
